Spin Deck™

Your next DJ app is free.

A full DJ app for your Mac: real decks, a real mixer, a memory for the mixes that worked, and stems made right on your computer.

  • Make Stems — split any song into drums, bass, other and vocals.
  • GOOD MIXX™ built in — save a good mix with SHIFT + ASSIST on the Inpulse T7.
  • Hercules Inpulse T7 support, plus ready-made setups for about 165 controllers.
  • Four real layout skins — BOOTH, CRATE, ON AIR and POCKET — plus color themes.
Download · Apple Silicon Intel Mac GitHub ↗ Report a problem

Built on Mixxx, the free and open-source DJ software (GPL-2.0-or-later). Spin Deck is open source too.

GOOD MIXX™

Remember the blends that work.

Ever nail a transition and then forget which two tracks did it? GOOD MIXX follows what you're playing in DJAY or TRAKTOR. Tap GOOD MIX when a blend works, and it suggests what to play next.

  • Follows DJAY and TRAKTOR Pro 4 or 3 on the Mac — no typing.
  • Your proven mixes first, then BPM + key matches.
  • STUDIO tab rebuilds your sets, and the set recorder captures them as played.
  • Saved on your Mac, never in the cloud. iPad works as a second screen.

Crate OS™

Your music. Your crates.

Crate OS scans your music library, helps you clean up the tags, and lets you build crates in play order — with GOOD MIXX in the same app.

  • Library scanning with tag review and safe, undoable tag writes.
  • Crate Builder: search, arrange in play order, save as M3U8 or CSV.
  • GOOD MIXX live decks, WHAT'S NEXT and saved mix memory built in.
  • Optional iPad or Meta Portal access on your own Wi-Fi.

Beatdeck™

        
Chop it. Play it. Flip it.

        
A free beat-making studio for your Mac. Chop any sample onto pads, play the chops as melodies, sequence two drum kits, and build it all into a song, right from your MIDI controller.

        
    
          
  • Waveform trimming, fades and 16 chop pads.
    • 
          
  • Melodic piano roll, two eight-piece drum kits, patterns and song mode.
    • 
          
  • M-Audio Code 25 first, plus Maschine MK2 and Alesis Vortex mappings.
    • 
          
  • Tempo sync with other apps through Ableton Link. WAV export.
